SignalShepherd

Import, clean, synchronise, cut, plot and export sensor and video data from an experiment

Screenshot or short screen recording

If you record force, pressure or position alongside video, lining the two up by hand takes hours. SignalShepherd does it in a few clicks and exports figures and clips ready for a paper or talk.

What it does

  1. 一Opens common sensor files and video side by side.
  2. 二Lines them up on one clock, then lets you cut out the parts you need.
  3. 三Exports plots, synced video and clean data files.
